# Third-party model assets

`run_video_pipeline.py` can download two files from the official
`opensim-org/opensim-moco` repository at commit
`492c0d08eef1b267ac9875579968b72c0f6d0fe8`:

- `Moco/Examples/C++/example2DWalking/2D_gait.osim`
- `Moco/Examples/C++/example2DWalking/referenceCoordinates.sto`

OpenSim Moco is distributed under the Apache License 2.0. Downloaded files are
stored under `models/` and are excluded from this repository.
